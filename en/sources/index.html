Sources and Rights

Where the information on Ritsumeikan OIC Map comes from, and who holds the rights.

Last updated: October 20, 2026

About this site

Ritsumeikan OIC Map is an unofficial service run by an individual. It is not provided or endorsed by Ritsumeikan University, the shops, or the transport operators. The information is compiled from the sources below and is not guaranteed to be accurate or up to date. Please check the official announcements for the latest information. See the Terms of Service for details.

Map (floor plans and facility locations)

This unofficial guide is processed and edited by the operator from Ritsumeikan University's "Osaka Ibaraki Campus Floor Guide (Japanese)", published in March 2025 by the OIC Regional Collaboration Office. The rights to the original materials, logos and names belong to their respective holders.

The locations of rooms and facilities, the pins, rings and areas, and the search data were compiled by the operator.

Food places (OIC Food)

Shop names, locations, hours and visitor information are written after checking the sources below. Hours can change. The check date is the day the operator last checked the source.

The shop descriptions are summaries, rewritten by the operator, of the descriptions on Ritsumeikan University's OIC Food Map (checked on October 16, 2026). The rights to the original text belong to its publisher.

The marks of each shop (the images on pins and shop cards) are original images made by the operator and are not the shops' official logos. The rights to shop names and brand names belong to their respective holders.

Today's food trucks (OIC Lunch Street)

The vendors and the days they set up come from the "OIC Lunch Street" calendar. The photos of each shop are cropped from the images in this calendar. The rights to the photos and shop names belong to each shop and the organizer.

ATMs on the map

The banks and hours shown for the ATMs on the map come from the "ATM service hours" table of Ritsumeikan University's Osaka Ibaraki Campus Floor Guide (issued March 2024) and from Sumitomo Mitsui Banking Corporation's branch and ATM guide (Ritsumeikan University Osaka Ibaraki Campus branch office).

Timetables and service status

The departure times on the timetable page are processed by the operator from the station timetable data each company publishes on its official pages (retrieved on October 16, 2026). The pages used are listed below.

The rights to the timetable data belong to each operator. Cancellations, extra trains, delays and platform changes may not be reflected. Please check each company's official announcement before you travel.

Service status is retrieved from the official pages of Osaka Monorail (osaka-monorail.co.jp), Hankyu (hankyu.co.jp/railinfo) and JR West (trafficinfo.westjr.co.jp). The rights to the service information also belong to each operator.

The departure times and service days of the shuttle bus (from OIC to BKC and Kinugasa) are transcribed by the operator of this site from the timetable and service-day PDFs on Ritsumeikan University's shuttle bus page (ritsumei.ac.jp/infostudents/shuttlebus/). The rights belong to Ritsumeikan University. Temporary cancellations and changes may not be reflected. Please check the university's official announcement before you ride.
